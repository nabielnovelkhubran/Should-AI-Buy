04. The Secret Sauce
Adversarial Intelligence

The Red Team: "What If We're Completely Wrong?"

How an adversarial agent prevents costly false breakouts and protects trading capital.

The Skeptic's Audit Checklist
1. Liquidity Trap & Order Book Depth
Is the volume spike organic, or is the asset too illiquid for clean entry/exit without devastating slippage?
2. Macro Regime Incompatibility
Does this breakout fight against macro market trend (e.g. attempting long breakout in strong BEAR market)?
3. Asymmetric Risk/Reward Ratio
Is the expected return at least 1.25x–2.0x larger than the technical invalidation stop distance?
Deliberation Outcome Matrix

Unlike traditional bots that binary-fire every moving average cross, our Synthesis Agent computes a consensus confidence score. If the Red Team discovers high contradictions, the candidate is instantly demoted to HOLD or REJECT.

Unanimous Strong Bull: ENTER (100% Size Cap)
Moderate Bull + Red Team Skeptic: SCALE (50% Sized Entry)
Unresolved Fatal Flaw / High Risk: PASS / REJECT ($0 Risked)
